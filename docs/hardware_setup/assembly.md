## Frame

The frame is assembled in 13 steps. In each drawing, the dashed magenta lines show where a part goes.
Steps 2 to 8 work on the bottom of the chassis, so turn it upside down after step 1.

### Step 1: Threaded inserts

![Step 1: four threaded inserts go into the corner posts on top of the chassis](images/frame/01-threaded-inserts.svg)

Press the four M2 brass threaded inserts into the holes of the corner posts on top of the chassis, for example with a soldering iron.
The decks are screwed into these inserts later, so they can be swapped often without wearing out the plastic threads.

**Parts:** chassis, 4 × M2 brass threaded insert

### Step 2: First motor

![Step 2: the first motor goes into its pocket on one side of the chassis](images/frame/02-first-motor.svg)

Turn the chassis upside down and place the first DG01D-E motor in its pocket on one side.

**Parts:** 1 × DG01D-E hobby motor with encoder

### Step 3: Second motor

![Step 3: the second motor goes into the pocket on the other side](images/frame/03-second-motor.svg)

Place the second motor in the pocket on the other side.

**Parts:** 1 × DG01D-E hobby motor with encoder

### Step 4: Motor screws

![Step 4: two long screws through each side wall and one screw into the end of each motor hold the motors](images/frame/04-motor-screws.svg)

Fix each motor with two M3 × 25 mm screws through the side wall of the chassis and the motor, and an M3 nut on each screw.
Then fix the far end of each motor with one more screw, which goes in along the motor through the end of the chassis.

**Parts:** 4 × M3 × 25 mm screw, 4 × M3 nut, 2 screws for the motor ends

### Step 5: Motor driver

![Step 5: the motor driver board goes between the motors](images/frame/05-motor-driver.svg)

Place the motor driver board between the two motors.

**Parts:** Adafruit DC Motor (+ Stepper) FeatherWing

### Step 6: Motor driver screws

![Step 6: four screws hold the motor driver board](images/frame/06-motor-driver-screws.svg)

Fix the board with four screws.

**Parts:** 4 × M2 self-tapping screw

### Step 7: Caster

![Step 7: the caster goes into the round opening of the chassis](images/frame/07-caster.svg)

Put the caster ball into the printed caster base and shroud, then place the caster in the round opening of the chassis.

**Parts:** caster base and shroud (printed), 1 × caster ball (25.4 mm)

### Step 8: Caster screws

![Step 8: four screws hold the caster](images/frame/08-caster-screws.svg)

Fix the caster with four screws.

**Parts:** 4 × M2 self-tapping screw

### Step 9: Battery pack

![Step 9: the battery pack slides into the compartment under the top plate](images/frame/09-battery-pack.svg)

Turn the chassis right side up again and slide the battery pack into the compartment under the top plate.

**Parts:** battery pack (for four or eight batteries)

### Step 10: Battery bracket

![Step 10: a bracket closes the battery compartment](images/frame/10-battery-bracket.svg)

Slide the bracket over the open side of the battery compartment, so the battery pack can't fall out.

**Parts:** battery bracket (printed)

### Step 11: Battery bracket screws

![Step 11: screws from the sides and the top hold the bracket](images/frame/11-battery-bracket-screws.svg)

Fix the bracket with screws from both sides and from the top.

**Parts:** 6 × M2 self-tapping screw

### Step 12: Powerbank

![Step 12: the powerbank goes on top of the chassis](images/frame/12-powerbank.svg)

Place the powerbank on top of the chassis. It can be at most 135.5 × 71 × 18 mm (L × W × H).

**Parts:** powerbank

### Step 13: Wheels

![Step 13: the wheels go onto the motor shafts](images/frame/13-wheels.svg)

Push the wheels onto the motor shafts.

**Parts:** 2 × wheel
